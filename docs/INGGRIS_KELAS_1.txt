BAGIAN A - MUDAH (Soal 1-10)

1. A teacher says, "Good morning." Rani answers politely and then says hello to Budi. Which word is a greeting?
   a. book
   b. red
   c. hello
   d. three

2. There are two red bags and one blue bag. Which sentence is true?
   a. There are two bags.
   b. There is one red bag.
   c. There are three bags.
   d. There are three blue bags.

3. Ali has a red pencil. Sari has a blue pencil. The color of Ali's pencil is ....
   a. blue
   b. green
   c. yellow
   d. red

4. Mother gives two books. Father gives one book. The English word for the total number is ....
   a. three
   b. one
   c. two
   d. four

5. A cat is under the chair and a dog is on the mat. Which animal is under the chair?
   a. cat
   b. dog
   c. bird
   d. fish

6. What is the English word for "lima"?
   a. four
   b. five
   c. six
   d. nine

7. What is the English word for "kucing"?
   a. dog
   b. fish
   c. cat
   d. bird

8. What is the English word for "buku"?
   a. bag
   b. ruler
   c. chair
   d. book

9. What is the English word for "mata"?
   a. eye
   b. ear
   c. nose
   d. hand

10. What is the English word for "tangan"?
   a. head
   b. hand
   c. eye
   d. foot
