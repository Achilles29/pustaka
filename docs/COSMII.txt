TEXT FOR QUESTIONS 6-10

My House

My house is a comfortable place where my family lives together. It has many different parts, and each part has a special purpose. The rooms are clean and organized so that we can do our daily activities easily.

The living room is the space where we gather to talk, watch television, or welcome guests. It has a big sofa, a table, and a bookshelf with many interesting books. Next to it is the dining room, where we eat meals together. The dining table is large enough for all family members to sit and enjoy food at the same time.

The bedroom is the most private part of the house. It is where we rest and sleep after a long day. My bedroom has a soft bed, a small desk for studying, and a wardrobe to keep my clothes neat. Every part of the house helps make our life more comfortable and organized.
